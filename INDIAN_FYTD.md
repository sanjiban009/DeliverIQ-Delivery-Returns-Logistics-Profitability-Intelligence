# Indian financial year and FYTD

This edition uses **FY 2026-27**, which runs from **1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027**.

The included FYTD dispatch cohort runs from **1 April 2026 through 16 November 2026**, inclusive. The cutoff is fixed for reproducibility and does not move when the file is opened later.

| Fiscal quarter | Months | Coverage in this edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | April–June | Complete |
| Q2 | July–September | Complete |
| Q3 | October–December | Partial, through 16 November |
| Q4 | January–March | Not yet in FYTD; no records |

Fiscal month numbers start at April = 1 and finish at March = 12. All charts are ordered chronologically from April. November is partial; do not infer a decline from its lower total relative to a complete month. No December–March actuals or forecasts are invented.

Financial_Year, Fiscal_Quarter and Fiscal_Month are derived from Order_Date in Excel (AH:AJ). The HTML quarter filter applies to every dashboard page alongside month, state, courier, payment and product filters. The default unfiltered dashboard and Excel Overview show the full included FYTD cohort.

The 20,000 synthetic rows have been regenerated within this fiscal window; this is not merely a relabelling of the old October–September dataset. Monetary results therefore differ from the previous edition. Synthetic closed-cohort terminal outcomes are used to illustrate analysis; this is a dispatch-cohort model, not a live status or transaction-recognition report as of 16 November. Some simulated eventual returns may occur after the dispatch cutoff.

All money is INR. The dashboard uses Indian digit grouping and lakh/crore abbreviations. Scenario savings cover the selected FYTD cohort, not a full financial year or automatic annual forecast. No prior-year dataset is included, so no YoY comparison is claimed.

The Financial Year slicer is populated from dataset values and filters every page, insight, scenario and CSV export. This dataset contains FY 2026-27 only; All financial years therefore gives the same total. Additional years are not fabricated.
